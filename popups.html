Rules that keep it welcome
Opens once per session and never again after dismissal. Waits until the visitor stops scrolling for 1.5 seconds. Never covers the booking calendar on mobile. Suppressed on checkout and on any page reached from an email link already answered.
Where the lead lands
Every conversation writes to one record: name, phone, program interest, source page and the answers given. Immediate text back with the class time and address. Coach sees it in the daily queue, so nothing waits in an inbox.
Why per page matters
A kid page visitor and a Muay Thai visitor want different things. Matching the opener to the page lifts engagement, and it tells you which page actually produces members.
